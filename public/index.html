What we do

We study the Earth as a complex and dynamic system. Using satellite-based Remote Sensing and Earth Observation techniques, combined with AI capabilities to better understand and predict changes in land, forest, water and ice. Our goal is to turn measurements into knowledge that industry, authorities and the scientific community can reliably act on.

What we are interested in

  • Microwave Remote Sensing
  • Satellite Earth Observation
  • Agentic AI
  • Monitoring of Forest, Land, Agriculture
