What we do

We study the Earth as a changing system. Using geodetic measurements, satellite Earth observation and machine learning, we measure how land, ice, water and cities move and change, and we turn those measurements into knowledge that planners, authorities and other researchers can act on.

What we are interested in

  • Geodesy and positioning
  • Satellite Earth observation
  • Machine learning for geodata
  • Land subsidence and deformation
  • Ice, water and sea level
  • Changing cities
